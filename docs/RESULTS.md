## Notes so far
- **E0 (oracle)** gives perfect step, order and video metrics on originals and truncations for all three step sets, so
  ground truth, truncation, checker and metrics agree. With default smoothing (2 fps, 5 s minimum run) the oracle loses a
  little: review rate 0.02 and 0.08 false alarms per compliant video. Both come from the anti-vibration handle, which
  completes about 2 s before the recording ends (D-006).
- **E1 (time prior)** marks every step done by the end of every video, truncated ones included. Its not-done recall is
  therefore 0, and any visual scorer must beat it. Its timing error (27 s MAE) is the bar for completion-time estimates.
- Order-violation support is small (test originals: 4 grouped violations), so order precision and recall are flagged
  as unreliable.

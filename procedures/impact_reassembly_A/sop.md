# SOP: Angle grinder reassembly (Fein CG15-125BL, IMPACT Model A)

> **Status: reviewed by the user (2026-11-04).** IMPACT does not release the instruction
> manual as text. This SOP is written from the IMPACT step labels (PSR, TAS-S) and the pictorial
> "Manual Book" figure on the IMPACT project page, which shows the standardized installation order.

## Starting state
The disassembled angle grinder parts lie in labelled bins on the work table: the gearbox housing,
the rotor assembly (drive shaft with bevel gear), the adapter plate with screws and M4 nuts, the
bearing plate assembly with screws, the locking lever assembly (lever, spring, washer, screw) and the
anti-vibration handle. Tools: screwdrivers and an open-end wrench.

## Steps
1. **Install the rotor assembly in the gearbox housing (G1).** Insert the drive shaft with the bevel
   gear into the gearbox housing. Tighten the M6 nut, first by hand and then with the wrench.
2. **Fasten the adapter plate (G2).** Requires step 1. Place the adapter plate on the housing and
   fasten both screws (top-left and lower-right) with their M4 nuts.
3. **Insert and fasten the bearing plate assembly (G3).** Requires step 2. Push the bearing plate
   assembly into the housing and fasten both bearing screws.
4. **Install the locking lever assembly (G4).** Requires step 3. Mount the locking lever with its
   spring and washer and tighten the lever screw.
5. **Screw on the anti-vibration handle (G5).** Requires step 1, since it screws into the gearbox housing.
   The manual shows it as the last step; doing it earlier is not treated as an order violation.
6. Return the tools to their place.

## Completion
All 17 components are installed: gearbox housing, drive shaft, bevel gear, M6 nut, adapter plate,
2 adapter screws, 2 M4 nuts, bearing plate, 2 bearing screws, lever screw, spring, lever, washer
and anti-vibration handle.
